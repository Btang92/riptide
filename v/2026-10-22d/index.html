What it changed

    

    
How it learns: probabilities move toward what actually happened in each 5-point confidence band, slowly at first (the model's own number counts as 25 calls until real results outweigh it), and calls against 5-minute momentum get their own correction. A band gets its calls flipped only when it has 60+ calls and wins clearly under 50% (more than 2 standard errors). Hour blocks, entry bands and trend states with 30+ priced calls get blocked when they lose more than 2¢ per contract after fees by more than their noise (the ± number); losing but not yet proven shows as watch. Blocks lift on their own when results recover. History replay uses Coinbase prices, so it is close to Kalshi's BRTI settlement but not identical. "Replay" and "would now avoid" are scored on the same calls the engine learned from, so they flatter it; trust the live P&L going forward.
