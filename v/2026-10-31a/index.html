What it changed

    

    
How it learns: probabilities move toward what actually happened in each 5-point confidence band, slowly at first (the model's own number counts as 25 calls until real results outweigh it), and calls against 5-minute momentum get their own correction. A band gets its calls flipped only when it has 60+ calls and wins clearly under 50% (more than 2 standard errors). Hour blocks, entry bands and trend states with 30+ priced calls get blocked when they lose more than 2¢ per contract after fees by more than their noise (the ± number); losing but not yet proven shows as watch. Blocks lift on their own when results recover. History replay uses Coinbase prices, so it is close to Kalshi's BRTI settlement but not identical. "Replay" and "would now avoid" are scored on the same calls the engine learned from, so they flatter it; trust the live P&L going forward.

Prices. Every button is a real Kalshi contract that pays $1 if it wins. The big number is the Kalshi price in American odds (−110, +150…), the small number is the price in cents. Kalshi's fee (up to 1.75¢) is included in every edge and payout shown.

    
Bet types. Moneyline = team wins. Spread −4.5 = must win by 5+; +4.5 = lose by 4 or fewer, or win. Total = combined points over/under the line. Team total = one team's points. Props = a player's stat, e.g. "15+ points" or "under 14.5 points". Unders and underdog spreads are the NO side of the Kalshi market.

    
Fair chance & edge. RIPTIDE prices every bet against outside markets with their built-in margins removed, weighting the sharpest most: Pinnacle (with an Odds API key, for moneylines, spreads and totals) counts 4×, Polymarket 2×, the median of other sportsbooks 1.5×, DraftKings (via ESPN) 1×. Edge = fair chance minus what Kalshi charges, in points. 
Expert picks 📣. Once a day RIPTIDE collects published expert picks from the web and marks matching bets. They never make a bet VALUE on their own; the closing-line check in Paper bets shows whether they actually help. 
Closing line. For every paper bet RIPTIDE keeps checking the price until the game starts. Beating the closing price consistently is the fastest proof that picks have an edge, long before wins and losses settle it. The chip under each price: +2.1 value · −0.4 about fair · ⚠ sharp money or news against it · — no outside price to compare.

    
Sharp money. Positions held by Polymarket's most profitable sports bettors (verified profit, month + all time), big Kalshi bets ($250+) in the last 24 hours, and DraftKings line movement since open. Tap any leg in your slip to see the details, injuries and news.

    
Parlays. All legs must win. The slip multiplies the leg prices; Kalshi's own combo price can be different, so compare before buying. Legs from the same game are linked (one result affects the other), so their combined odds are less reliable.
